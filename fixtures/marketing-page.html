What it does

Real boxes

Geometry comes from the browser's own layout, so what you see is what lands on the canvas.

Auto layout

Flex and grid containers arrive as auto layout frames, gaps and padding intact.

Tokens

Custom properties become Figma variables, and matching colours are bound to them.
